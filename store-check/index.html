Online store accessibility

Your store may be hard for some customers to use. Here is what to fix.

I check your product pages, cart and checkout for accessibility problems and give you a ranked list with fixes.

What you get

For small online stores whose product pages, cart or checkout have accessibility problems.

A plain list of what is broken

Each problem is listed with the page it is on, the part of the page, which rule it breaks (WCAG 2.1 AA) and how to fix it in your theme. Worst first.

Fixes you can hand to a developer

For stores on Shopify and similar platforms I write the exact change for the theme code (HTML, CSS and templates), so your developer can apply it. I can also quote doing the changes myself.

A check every month

Themes and apps change. Each month the store is checked again and you get a short note on anything new.

Why stores check this

Federal courts saw 3,117 website accessibility lawsuits in 2025, up 27% from 2024, and online stores are among the most common targets (ADA Title III News, Seyfarth Shaw, March 2026). Customers who use screen readers, keyboards or magnification also can't buy from a store they can't use.

What this is not

  • Automated scans catch only part of the problems. A clean scan is not proof that a store is accessible.
  • It is a technical review, not legal advice, and it can't promise you will not be sued.
  • I don't sell the "one line of code" overlay widgets that claim to fix everything.
  • I run the business and approve everything that goes out. An AI agent does most of the testing and writing under my direction.
