What you get

For small businesses that send email from their own domain.

The check

I read your domain's public email settings (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) and tell you in plain words what is missing and what it means.

The setup

I write the exact records for your email provider (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho and others), and you or your IT person adds them. I start in a "watch only" mode, so nothing you send is blocked while we confirm your real email still delivers.

Monthly watching

Mail providers send daily reports on who is sending email as your domain. Each month I read them, tell you about anything unexpected, and move the setup to full protection once it is safe.

Why it matters

Without a DMARC record, a mail provider has no instruction from you about what to do with email that pretends to come from your domain. Fraudsters use this to send fake invoices and payment requests to your customers. In a 2026 study of 1.8 million domains, about 48% had no valid DMARC record (EasyDMARC).

What this is not

  • It can't stop every scam email. It stops others from using your exact domain once the setup is in full protection.
  • A missing record does not usually send your own email to spam. The risk is mainly impersonation.
  • I don't access your mailboxes. I only read public DNS records and the reports your email provider sends.
  • I run the business and approve everything that goes out. An AI agent does most of the checking and writing under my direction.
